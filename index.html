07 Straight answers

The questions people actually ask, answered before they have to.

What stops someone writing lore that is a lie?
Nothing stops them writing it. What the seal does is stop them changing it later. A coin that claimed one thing in January and something else in June is, right now, impossible to catch. Here it is trivial — the seal will not match. Permanence is not a truth machine, it is an accountability machine.
Does the founder need a wallet to claim the scribe share?
They need one to receive it, not to earn it. The share accrues from the first trade regardless. They claim it by posting their seal from the handle it was signed with, which anyone can verify because the seal is derived from the handle itself. If they never claim it, it stays escrowed. It does not come back to us.
Why FNV-1a and not a real cryptographic hash?
Because you should be able to compute it by hand. FNV-1a is four lines of code, it is in the docs, and it runs in your browser here with no server call. It is a tamper-evidence check, not a security primitive — the chain is what makes it binding, the hash just makes an edit obvious.
Where do the numbers on this page come from?
Handles and avatars are read live from X. Holders come from the Robinhood Chain explorer. Price, market cap and 24h volume come from Dexscreener. Paired stock prices come from public market data. Anywhere a number does not exist yet, the page shows a dash instead of filling it in.
Is the coin live?
Not yet. When it is, the contract address will be at the top of this page and in the pinned post. Anyone posting a contract address before it appears here is scamming you.
